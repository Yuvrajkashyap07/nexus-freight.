# Selected research sources

Research snapshot: 9 November 2026. Links are provided for the professor to verify the original material. The summaries below are paraphrases, not direct quotations.

1. **McKinsey, 8 May 2026 — Code and cargo: How AI could change freight logistics.** Discusses AI-fuelled freight brokerage/forwarding software and examples of automation across quotation, order acceptance, check calls, tracking and invoice handling. Also warns that AI may weaken software-interface moats and make data/integration advantages more important.
   https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/logistics/our-insights/code-and-cargo-how-ai-could-change-freight-logistics

2. **McKinsey, 8 September 2026 — Creating value from AI and digital capabilities in logistics operations.** Explores what logistics organisations do differently when they get business value from AI and digital capability.
   https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/creating-value-from-ai-and-digital-capabilities-in-logistics-operations

3. **DHL Group, 24 September 2026 — Logistics Trend Radar 8.0.** Identifies agentic AI, AI analytics, AI ethics, cybersecurity, workforce changes, resilience, decarbonisation and other long-horizon logistics trends.
   https://group.dhl.com/en/media-relations/press-releases/2026/ai-takes-action-while-people-remain-at-the-center-of-logistics-finds-dhl-logistics-trend-radar.html

4. **DCSA — Track & Trace standard.** Defines a common event language for shipment, transport, equipment, IoT and reefer visibility events and explains why inconsistent event data creates manual reconciliation and delay.
   https://dcsa.org/standards/track-and-trace

5. **DCSA — Industry Blueprint 2026.Q1.** Overview of shipment, equipment and vessel journeys, including booking, documentation, cargo movement, release and invoice settlement.
   https://reference.dcsa.org/content/standards/industry-blueprint/current/industry-blueprint

6. **DCSA — Shipment Journey 2026.Q1.** End-to-end shipment journey across booking, documentation, release and invoice settlement.
   https://reference.dcsa.org/content/standards/industry-blueprint/current/industry-blueprint-shipment-journey

7. **IATA — ONE Record.** Explains the digital cargo single-record vision and standardised, secured API data sharing.
   https://www.iata.org/one-record

8. **IATA Cargo — ONE Record release note, July 2026.** Notes the endorsed Ontology 3.3.0 and API 2.3.0 release; states ONE Record's endorsed role as preferred air-cargo data-sharing standard from 1 January 2026.
   https://iata-cargo.github.io/ONE-Record/2026-07/General/release-note/

9. **Streamlit — Community Cloud deployment.** Official deployment workflow and application structure guidance.
   https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/deploy-your-app

10. **Streamlit — Secrets management.** Recommends keeping secrets out of source repositories and adding them through the app settings.
    https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/deploy-your-app/secrets-management

11. **Streamlit — Manage your app / resource limits.** Describes Community Cloud resource limits and operational behaviours; limits may change.
    https://docs.streamlit.io/deploy/streamlit-community-cloud/manage-your-app

## Research limitations

- The market research above is selected to validate the workflow and standards direction, not to claim a precise global TAM.
- McKinsey and DHL sources include industry perspectives and selected use cases, not independently audited benchmarks for every forwarder.
- Vendor product pages are not neutral comparisons. Validate pricing, integration rights and coverage directly during procurement.
- The MVP uses synthetic demo transactions. No customer interviews, paid pilot or production integration has yet validated NEXUS's commercial assumptions.
